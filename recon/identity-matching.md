## What this pairing still cannot see

- Unnamed private leaf helpers with no literal and no RTTI slot: only the
  strict structural fallback can reach them, and that is LOW confidence.
- A wrong NAME can still mispair: recovered names come from `__FUNCSIG__`
  and RTTI, so a function that merely logs another function's signature
  is mis-named. Aliases are hand-maintained and stated above.
- ICF/thunks: identical bodies are intentionally shared; they are counted
  as pairs under the `icf` anchor and listed separately, not silently.
- The strict structural fallback is deliberately conservative (>=0.90
  token ratio, same instruction count, mutual best). It REJECTS rather
  than guesses, so some true counterparts remain MISSING.
